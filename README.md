Gaurav Kumar, known professionally as **The Algothrim**, is a founder, digital artist, and full-stack developer from New Delhi. He runs **The Outfit House** and **Ruach's Farms**.

I work on both sides of a product, the look and the engineering, because I don't think they should live apart. Most of what is here I built for my own businesses, my family, or my own life, and then made properly.
